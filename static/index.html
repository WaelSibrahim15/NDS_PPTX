Design

Choose the template and language. Claude drafts the slides first; narration comes after you approve them.

Claude treats these as the highest rule, above its own defaults, and checks the draft against them before you see it. They also apply when you redraft, regenerate a slide, or rewrite narration.

03

Review the slides

Edit titles and content, change layouts, reorder, or ask Claude to redo a slide. Download the slides as they are, or approve them to add a voice.

Changes save automatically
Not happy with the draft? Replaces every slide with a fresh draft that follows this instruction and your instructions in step 02 (edit them there first if needed). You can undo once.
